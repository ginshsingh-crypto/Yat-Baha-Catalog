---
name: Community safety decisions
description: Privacy boundaries when moving the device-only product to shared reporting.
---
Do not silently upload legacy device data or assign it to the currently signed-in account. Publishing a legacy report must be an explicit copy action.

**Why:** Existing users were promised device-only storage; signing in is not consent to publish photos and descriptions.

**How to apply:** Preserve local originals and clearly disclose publication before migration or sync.

Contact acceptance is not verified ownership. Human moderation must approve messages before the other party receives them; no first-signup moderator privileges.

**Why:** The service involves strangers claiming valuable property; automatic trust or contact disclosure would undermine the requested safety controls.

**How to apply:** Keep authorization server-side, private evidence out of discovery, and moderator grants operator-controlled.
